What are we

CollegeAPP is an online tool used to calculate your chances of getting into colleges, using info gathered from grades, GPAs, location, and other statistics to give the most accurate possible guess of if you will get in!

How does it work

CollegeAPP works by collecting info from you such as:

  • GPA
  • Location
  • School History
and comparing it to stats from a college such as:
  • Acceptance rate
  • Location
  • Requirements
